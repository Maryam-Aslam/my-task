## Which JLR Engine Codes Are Commonly Confused?

- **204DTD / 204DTA / 204DTH / 204DTF:** 204DTF is handled as a catalogue alias/reference within our 204DTD guidance, not a separate priced engine product. 204DTH remains a distinct enquiry route. Shared parts do **not** establish full-engine interchangeability.
- **204PT versus PT204:** a Ford-era 2.0 turbo petrol application is not automatically interchangeable with a later 2.0 Ingenium petrol.
- **306DT versus DT306:** the older 3.0 V6 diesel and the newer 3.0 Ingenium straight-six diesel are different engine architectures.
- **306PS versus 508PS:** supercharged Jaguar petrol engines of different displacement and arrangement cannot be selected merely from the badge.
- **448DT versus BMW-sourced 4.4 V8:** a 4.4-litre diesel V8 and a BMW-sourced 4.4-litre petrol V8 are unrelated.
- **PHEV vehicles:** the advertised P300e/P440e/P550e badge can reflect the combined hybrid system, not the combustion engine's standalone output.

We identify engines from more than the registration where necessary; photographs of the code label, build information and the actual assembly may be needed.

## Common Engine Failure Symptoms — What Should You Check First?

Rattling on start-up, oil-pressure warnings, excessive smoke, coolant loss, overheating and reduced power can point to engine damage—but they can also involve ancillary systems. Diagnostic testing prevents replacing the wrong component.

Relevant service pathways include [timing-chain repair](/engine-inspection/), [timing-belt repair](/engine-inspection/), [turbocharger repair](/engine-inspection/), [cylinder-head repair](/engine-inspection/), [head-gasket repair](/engine-inspection/) and [engine inspection](/engine-inspection/).
