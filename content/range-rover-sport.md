## Range Rover Sport Engine Notes: What to Know Before You Order

- **The 2021 update changed the engines.** The V6 and V8 diesels gave way to six-cylinder D250 / D300 / D350 engines.
- **5.0 V8:** the supercharged engine (508PS) in the L494 is not the BMW-sourced 4.4 V8 in the L461.
- **3.0 V6 diesel (306DT):** a front camshaft belt, rear fuel-pump belt and internal chains. Belts are due at 7 years or 112,000 miles.
- **P400e:** a 2.0 petrol plus electric motor. We work on the combustion engine only.

| Overheating or coolant loss (any engine) | Temperature rising, coolant loss, white smoke, milky oil | [Head and block sealing](/engine-inspection/), coolant pipes, water pump, pressure testing |

## Range Rover Sport Engine FAQs

### What does a Range Rover Sport engine rebuild cost?

Rebuilds start from £5,994 and run up to £8,394. The 3.0 V6 diesel is £5,994 and the 5.0 supercharged V8 is £8,394. Other engines are priced from your registration.

### What does supply and fit cost for a Range Rover Sport?

From £6,660 to £9,300 for the engines listed. a replacement supplied and fitted by our workshop carries a 12-month unlimited-mileage warranty, subject to written terms.

### Which Range Rover Sport engine do I have?

It depends on the generation, year and badge. Enter your registration and we confirm the engine code; the 2021 update changed most engines.

### Can I buy a Range Rover Sport engine without fitting?

Yes. Choose Engine only and Request Current Price & Availability. We don’t publish engine-only prices because availability changes them.

### Should I rebuild or replace my Range Rover Sport engine?

In many cases rebuilding your own engine is the better route: it keeps your engine number and avoids an unknown donor history. We can’t promise a rebuild until we inspect the engine.

### Do you work on Range Rover Sports brought from Europe?

Yes. We regularly work on vehicles brought over or recovered from Europe. European-market vehicles can carry engines not sold in the UK, so we confirm from the VIN and engine number. Recovery is quoted individually.

### How much does a Range Rover Sport V8 engine rebuild cost?

The 5.0 supercharged V8 is at the top of the range: £8,394 for a rebuild and £9,300 supplied and fitted (L494 2014–2022, including SVR).

### Did the Range Rover Sport change engines in 2021?

Yes. The V6 and V8 diesels were replaced by 3.0 six-cylinder D250, D300 and D350 engines, and the petrol range changed.

### Does the 3.0 V6 diesel have a timing belt or chain?

Both. It uses a front camshaft belt, a rear fuel-pump belt and short internal chains. The belts are due at 7 years or 112,000 miles, subject to your vehicle’s schedule.
